# Privacy Policy

**App:** X-hale — Quit Smoking
**Last updated:** 2026-06-15

---

## What data we collect

X-hale collects only the data necessary to support your quit journey:

- **Account information** — email address and password (used for authentication only).
- **Quit profile** — quit date, cigarettes per day, years smoking, pack price. You provide this during onboarding.
- **Diary entries** — text entries you write in the app.
- **Chat history** — your conversations with Hailey, the in-app AI assistant.
- **Hailey memory** — structured facts Hailey extracts from your conversations (e.g. known triggers, coping strategies that work for you, emotional patterns). You can view and delete these at any time from the "What does Hailey know about me?" screen.
- **Usage milestones and achievements** — tracked locally and synced to your account.

We do not collect device identifiers, location data, or any data unrelated to smoking cessation.

---

## How we use your data

- To personalise Hailey's responses based on your quit history and known patterns.
- To display your progress (days smoke-free, money saved, health benefits reached).
- To send optional push notifications (reminders, milestone alerts) if you enable them.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

---

## Where your data is stored

All personal data is stored on **Appwrite Cloud**, which encrypts data at rest using AES-256. Appwrite Cloud infrastructure is hosted on Google Cloud Platform (europe-west region).

Conversations with Hailey are processed in real time by **Groq** (LLM inference) and **Qdrant Cloud** (vector search over anonymised medical knowledge base content). Neither service retains your conversation data after the request completes.

---

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **View** all data Hailey has collected about you — via the "What does Hailey know about me?" screen in the app.
- **Delete individual facts** — from the same screen, tap the delete icon next to any fact.
- **Export your data** — contact us at the address below and we will provide a full export within 30 days.
- **Delete all your data** — contact us at the address below to request complete erasure of your account and all associated data.

If you are located in the European Union, these rights are granted under GDPR (Articles 15–17, 20).

---

## Data retention

Chat history and Hailey memory facts are retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently erased within 30 days.

---

## Children

X-hale is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
