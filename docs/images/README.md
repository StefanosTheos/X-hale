## Cropping and image fidelity

The seven full-device sources are 1080 × 2340 pixels. Their retained rectangle
is `(left=0, top=96, right=1080, bottom=2200)`, with exclusive right/bottom bounds.
The 1080 × 2104 exports omit the phone's clock/battery/notifications and Android
three-button navigation bar. Application headers, controls and app navigation
remain intact.

`ongoing challenges.jpg` was supplied as an already cropped 1080 × 851 panel
with no device bars. Its full area is retained, showing both a running goal and
a savings goal. It replaces the former single financial-challenge card.

All eight exports use lossless WebP at their retained native resolution.
Decoded export pixels match the decoded source crop exactly. No UI text,
illustrations or values have been replaced, retouched or generated. The complete
gallery totals 2,200,130 bytes. Lead screens display at 240 pixels wide;
expanded full screens at 300 pixels and the challenge panel at 480 pixels.
Every image links to its full-resolution file. Original source files are unchanged.

## Provenance and interpretation

Exact capture dates and application/deployment revisions were not supplied.
Visible application dates do not establish a precise capture time or backend
revision; the preparation date above records the gallery update only.

The captures illustrate the application interface, not benchmark results or
clinical effectiveness. Health-benefit timelines and progress are informational,
not measurements of individual physiological recovery. The stretching capture
shows the existing routine interface and instructions. Asset permissions remain
separate work in [third-party notices](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Raw `Pics/` captures remain excluded from routine commits. Future captures should
exclude personal information, credentials and phone notifications, and record
the application revision. Biometrics captures must identify demonstration data.
